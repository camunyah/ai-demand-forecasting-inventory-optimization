# 📈 Executive Dashboards

The Official Retail Reference Implementation concludes with executive decision artifacts that transform enterprise intelligence into actionable business recommendations.

Rather than ending with predictive models, CEDEF culminates in executive dashboards that support organizational planning, governance, and strategic decision-making.

---

## Enterprise Predictive Capability Dashboard

Provides a comparative evaluation of multiple predictive capabilities engineered throughout the framework, enabling executive selection of the most appropriate production forecasting capability.

---

## Production Forecast Dashboard

Transforms certified predictive capabilities into operational production forecasts that support enterprise planning and inventory management.

---

## Inventory Planning Dashboard

Converts production forecasts and inventory intelligence into enterprise replenishment recommendations that support operational decision-making.

Evaluates the financial implications of proposed inventory strategies by balancing inventory holding costs against shortage costs and enterprise service objectives.
